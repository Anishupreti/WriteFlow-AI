Let's connect your AI

WriteFlow doesn't have a server of its own — you connect your own API key, and your writing goes straight from your browser to the AI provider you pick. Nothing passes through us.

Getting a key takes about a minute, and a couple of options are completely free with no credit card. We'll walk you through it.

Step 1 of 3

Pick an AI provider

Any of these will work. Gemini and Groq are free with no credit card, so they're the easiest place to start.

Step 2 of 3

Get your key

This is saved only on this device, never sent anywhere except directly to the provider you picked.

Step 3 of 3

Checking your key…

We're just confirming the provider recognizes it. This doesn't cost anything or use up your free quota.

You're all set

Click into any text box on the web — an email, a comment, a message — and a small icon will appear. Click it (or press Ctrl+Shift+Space, ⌘+Shift+Space on Mac) to write, rewrite, or reply with AI.

    Privacy & Data

    Writing samples, style notes, your API key, and settings are stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local). If you verify a Pro licence, your licence key is sent to Gumroad. WriteFlow has no AI proxy server and does not receive your writing requests.
